# Logic review of the current implementation (2026-10-20)

Scope: the web money, ticket, sync, confirmation and backup code, and the matching Smart210 code (upload, purge, spot sale, supplies). Read only; nothing was run against real hardware and the Qt screens were not looked at. `AUDIT.md` (2026-10-10) is not repeated: items it closed by procedure or by decision are only mentioned where the code now disagrees with the decision. Paths are from the repository root.

Severity: **A** = can lose or misstate money or data, fix before go-live; **B** = a rule that is inconsistent or missing, decide and fix; **C** = small.

## A. Fix before go-live

**A1. The backup clears records that nobody confirmed. FIXED 2026-10-23:** `BackupRun::blockers` refuses the backup (and the Backup page shows why) while a sale or supplement it would clear is not confirmed, or a terminal has not uploaded since its business day ended (`backup.business_day_start_hour`). The original finding: `BackupRun::clearable` (`web/app/Support/Admin/BackupRun.php:125`) selects sales, supplements, fuel inbounds and used tickets by date only. It never looks at `confirmed_at`, and the controller only requires the date to be before today (`web/app/Http/Controllers/Admin/BackupController.php:33`). Unconfirmed sales and supplements of those days are removed from the live database (they stay only in the zip), the accountant can no longer review them, and a terminal that uploads a day later re-creates them. Suggested rule: refuse (or list) when any sale or supplement up to the date has `confirmed_at` null, and when a device still has records for that date unsent.

**A2. A return rewrites the debt of the original sale. FIXED 2026-10-23:** a return adds a negative `debt_adjustments` row dated the day of the return; `debts.amount` is never changed, `Debt::net_amount` is the two together, and the debt summary shows the cut on the day of the return. The original finding: `SaleReturn::create` reduces `debts.amount` in place (`web/app/Support/Sales/SaleReturn.php:92`). The debt summary dates a debt by its sale and sums `debts.amount` (`web/app/Http/Controllers/Debts/DebtSummaryController.php:79`), so the opening and closing figures of days that were already confirmed change afterwards. This breaks the stated principle that a return is a counter entry that leaves confirmed days as they were. Suggested: keep `debts.amount` and record the cut as a negative entry dated today (a debt adjustment row), the way the return sale row itself works.

**A3. A wrong debt payment or loan settlement cannot be corrected. FIXED 2026-10-24:** the Payments and Ticket Loans pages list the payments and the tickets surrendered, each with a Reverse button (accountant): it adds a negative line dated today (`debt_payments.reverses_payment_id`, `ticket_loan_settlements.reverses_settlement_id`), the wrong line stays, a settlement's ticket goes back to the customer. A payment cannot be dated ahead; one over the balance is kept, a warning says so and the debt shows a Credit badge. A sale whose payments were all reversed can be deleted or returned again. The original finding: The routes only create (`payments.create/store`, `loans.create/store`), and the amount must be at least 0.01 (`DebtController.php:98`). There is no delete, edit or reversal. The payment date has no upper limit (`DebtController.php:101`, the sale forms use `before_or_equal:today`), and a payment tied to a debt may exceed its balance without a warning. Suggested: a reversal entry (like the card ledger has), a date limit, and a visible credit when a payment is larger than the balance.

**A4. Tickets can be stored as sold when their sale was rejected. FIXED 2026-10-23:** `Push::requireRecords` refuses a ticket (sold or supplied) whose sale or supplement is not on the server; it waits and is accepted on a later upload once the record is. A ticket that is already sold is not checked again, so a backup that cleared the sale does no harm. The original finding: `Push::tickets` (`web/app/Support/Sync/Push.php:364`) only checks that a sold ticket names a sale id; it does not check that the sale exists. If a sale row is rejected (the validation at lines 175-196) while its tickets are accepted, the tickets are marked sold with a `sale_id` that points nowhere: the stock is gone and no money is recorded. Checked on the terminal side: `Uploader::sendNext` (`smart210/src/sync/Uploader.cpp:400-420`) leaves out only the refused keys of the same table, so the tickets of a refused sale are still sent. The safe rule on the web side is to reject a sold ticket whose sale is not stored (the push order already puts sales first).

**A5. A card fill is refused after the fuel was dispensed. FIXED 2026-10-23:** the fill is stored, the balance goes below zero, the case is recorded once among the sync problems, and the Cards page lists the cards below zero in a warning and shows their balance in red. The original finding: `Push::supplements` refuses a card supply that would take the card below zero (`Push.php:278-284`). The terminal has already poured the fuel, so the row stays refused and retried, the ledger never gets the fill, and the customer had free fuel. For tickets the rule is the opposite: "fuel that was dispensed is a fact" (`Push.php:360-363`). Suggested: store the fill, let the balance go negative, and show the card in a warning list.

## B. Inconsistent or missing rules (decide)

**B1. Only sales and supplements can be confirmed.** Fuel inbounds (edit and delete, `FuelInboundController`), ticket inbounds, debt payments, ticket loans, remainders and the opening balances (`balances.manage`, `BalanceController`) stay editable at any date for ever, so a closed day's tank stock, debts and loans can change. The opening balances are not frozen after go-live. This is also what the day close of the next phase has to cover.

**B2. What "day is complete" means.** `ConfirmationController::store` accepts today (`before_or_equal:today`) and the dump is queued when the records the web knows are confirmed (`dayIsFullyConfirmed`). The web cannot see what a terminal still holds unsent for that date; `AUDIT.md` M1/H2 closed this by procedure. A cheap guard: warn when a device's last sync is older than the end of that business day.

**B3. The sale total is not checked against price and weight.** `Push::sales` checks paid + unpaid = total, but not total = price x kg, and the items' kg are not checked against the sale's kg (`Push.php:175-181`). The terminal can type an amount and derive the kg (`SpotSale::create`), so the two can differ by the rounding of the price. A mismatch beyond one cent per kg-price step would go through silently. The web forms compute the total on the server and are safe.

**B4. The 0.05 kg tolerance exists only on the terminal.** `SupplyRecord::ToleranceX100` (`smart210/src/supplies/SupplyRecord.h:29`) means a fill short of its preset by up to 0.05 kg leaves no remainder. The web manual supply (`SupplementController.php:193`) makes a remainder ticket for any positive difference, so litres x density rounding can produce a remainder of 0.01 to 0.04 kg. The same screen takes any `supplied_at` (no upper limit, line 104) and sets the tickets' `supply_date` to `now()` (line 168) while the supplement uses the date of `supplied_at` (line 161), so the two dates can differ.

**B5. Who can do what.** `web/config/permissions.php`: the accountant creates sales, debt payments and opening balances and also confirms them; the assistant publishes tickets, sells, deletes and returns sales. Segregation of duties is therefore only the audit log. Not a bug if intended; worth one explicit decision, with the audit screen as the control.

**B6. Dates.** A web sale can be dated into a day that is already confirmed and then shows up as an unconfirmed record in that day (allowed by design, with no warning). The terminal's business day (9:00 to 9:00, `Checkout::businessDay`) and the web calendar day differ; closed by `AUDIT.md` M1 by procedure.

## C. Small

- **C1 (closed 2026-10-25, not an issue).** Cards are never deleted, only blocked, so `Card::find()` cannot miss a card that a terminal knows. `Card::find()` returns nothing for a deleted card, so a card sale or card supply for it fails with "property of non-object" (`Push.php:187`, `262`, `280`). The row is reported as refused and retried; the message should say that the card is deleted.
- **C2 (closed 2026-10-25, not an issue).** The terminal's clock is set from the web server's at every download (a minute) and the footer warns above 60 s, so a wrong clock does not happen in practice. The card balance is a running total by `occurred_at`, then `seq` (`CardLedger::recompute`). A terminal with a wrong clock can show a negative balance in the history while the total is correct.
- **C3 (fixed 2026-10-25: a sale needs a price above 0 on the web forms and in the push; own use and other use may be free).** A web sale takes any price from 0 up (`SaleController.php`, `price` min 0); the fuel type's price is only the default in the form. A price of 0 on a `sale` gives a free sale without a warning.

## What was checked and is sound

Pushes are idempotent by key and stored one row per transaction; one bad row cannot block a batch (`PushController`). Ticket and sale stock is locked (`lockForUpdate`) in sale, return and delete. Money on the terminal is integer cents; rounding on both sides is half away from zero. Returns and deletes check confirmation, age, used tickets, payments and settlements. The card balance is a ledger with one entry per record and a reversal, not a stored counter. The backup runs a safety dump first and records the carry forward per tank.

## Suggested order

1. A1, A4, A5 (data and fuel facts), then A2, A3 (money history).
2. B1 together with the day close of the next phase.
3. B3 and B4 (consistency between the terminal and the web), then the small items.
