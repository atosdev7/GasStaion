## A. Can lose fuel or money, or hide it

**A1. A fill that overshoots its tickets is stuck for ever. NOT FIXED, by decision (B1, 2026-10-31):** an overshoot was briefly absorbed up to 0.05 kg on the branch; that tolerance was removed again, so the firmware of the board must never let a fill go past its preset. The original finding: `SupplySession` converts the board's grams to kg by rounding to the nearest 0.01 (`(g + 5) / 10`), so 5 g over the tickets already reads as 0.01 kg over. `SupplyRecord::record` then saves the supply without its tickets (message "saved without them") with `preset_kg` equal to the board's preset. `Push::supplements` refuses any row with `out_amount_kg > preset_kg`, so it is refused on every upload. The tickets stay sold and unused, so the customer keeps them and the station lost the fuel. The nozzle's valve lag makes small overshoots normal. Fix: treat an overshoot up to the same 0.05 kg tolerance as covered (use the tickets, the station absorbs it) on the terminal and in the push; list supplies without tickets on the Review screen.

**A2. The "terminal has uploaded" guard is set by the first request, not by a complete upload. FIXED on the branch:** the terminal reports `POST /api/sync/uploaded` (from the upload beat, every 5 minutes) when no record from before the running business day waits for an upload, refused ones included; `last_synced_at` is set by that call only. The original finding: `PushController::store` sets `devices.last_synced_at = now()` before it validates anything. `BackupRun::silentDevices` (the backup blocker, the dump after confirmation and the Overview warning) treats that as proof that the terminal has sent everything. An upload that stops after the first table, or a nozzles-only push, satisfies it while the sales and supplies of the day are still on the terminal. A nozzles-only push is not rare: `MainWindow::autoUpload` and `sendNozzles` send changed nozzles whatever the upload mode is, so a terminal in manual mode that had a nozzle edited after the day ended counts as uploaded with every sale still unsent. Fix: let the terminal tell the server when a full upload finished (a `push/done` call after the last table with nothing refused or waiting for the dates before the business day) and set `last_synced_at` only then.

**A3. Opening balances get different ids on the live and the backup server. RESOLVED on the branch by procedure (decided 2026-10-30):** the live server is initialised first and the whole site and database are then copied to the backup server, so the ids are the same; `backup:import` counts payments and settlements that point at an opening debt or loan the backup server lacks. Note that 9.4ew had said these payments would show there as "not tied to one"; the code never did that. The original finding: Debts, ticket loans and opening remainders are created with random UUIDs (`HasUuidPrimaryKey`). Since `7f767de` they are not exported and are "entered on both servers at go-live". The payments and settlements made against them are exported with the live server's ids, so on the backup server they point at nothing: the opening debts there never show a payment, and a settled opening debt that a backup cleared on the live server exists nowhere except the safety dump. Fix: give opening rows ids that are the same on both servers (a one-time export/import at go-live, or a deterministic id), or export them again.

**A4. Card balances are not counted as owed fuel in the ordering figures. FIXED on the branch:** `StockOrdering` adds the positive card balances per fuel type (`card_owed`) to what is owed; the Ordering report and its Excel export have a column for it and the dashboard says "tickets and cards". The original finding: `StockOrdering::rows` subtracts only tickets sold and not supplied (and the old paper tickets) from the tank stock. A card's balance is fuel that was paid for and must still be supplied, exactly like a ticket. The "free fuel" and the "days it lasts" on the dashboard and the Ordering report are too high by the sum of all card balances. Fix: add `CardLedger::balances()` (positive ones) per fuel type to `owed`.

**A5. A ticket the web already settled for a loan can be poured against with no trace. FIXED on the branch:** a conflict is logged whenever the pushed supplement differs from the stored one, including when one is empty. The original finding: `Push::tickets`, branch "already supplied": a conflict is logged only when both the stored and the pushed `supplement_id` are set. A ticket used for a loan on the web has none, so fuel poured afterwards leaves no conflict. Fix: log a conflict whenever the pushed supplement differs from the stored one, including when the stored one is empty.

**A6. A terminal sale is stored without checking its total. FIXED on the branch:** `Push::sales` refuses a total more than one cent plus half a hundredth of a kg of fuel away from price x kg, and items whose kg do not add up to the sale's. The original finding: `Push::sales` checks paid + unpaid = total but not total = price × kg, and not that the ticket items add up to the sale's kg (REVIEW 2026-10-20 B3, still open). Fix: refuse a total more than one cent plus half a hundredth of a kg of fuel away from price × kg; the items must add up to the weight.

**A7. The terminal's settlement can exceed a loan, and the web must keep it.** This is by design (the web shows a credit). Any new check on the web that refuses such a settlement leaves the ticket used on the terminal and unrecorded on the web, so do not add one.

## B. Rules that disagree between the web and the terminal

- **B1. Remainder tolerance. DECIDED 2026-10-31: no tolerance anywhere.** The terminal, the push, the card refund and the supply check of the confirmation all use zero (to the hundredth of a kg); the firmware of the board makes the fill stop at the preset. This also reverses the 0.05 kg overshoot allowance of A1. The original finding: The terminal makes no remainder for a shortfall up to 0.05 kg (when the fill ended at its preset). `SupplementController::returnRemainder` makes a remainder ticket for any positive difference, and `CardLedger::refund` ignores up to 0.05. The same fill gives different results by entry route.
- **B2. A card ticket's unused part.** The web gives it back to the card (`CardLedger::refund`); the terminal makes a paper remainder ticket for it (`SupplyRecord::record` does not look at the ticket's card). Same kg, different instrument.
- **B3. Default cost at upload time.** `Push::sales` and `Push::supplements` take `DefaultCost::get()` when the row arrives, not when it happened, and the comment in `Push` still says otherwise. After a day offline and a changed default the margin drifts (the accountant can correct it before confirming).
- **B4. Direct set leaves the fuel with no paper.** With "Set directly" the sale (or the card fill) is saved and the ticket goes straight into the nozzle's queue, unprinted. If the supplier never takes the gun up, or the queue is cleared at the dispenser (`FlagClearKey`), the ticket stays sold and unprinted, and the spot-sale screen's "Print again" is gone once the screen changes. Check that a reprint path exists for a spot-sale ticket outside the remainders list.
- **B5. Opening balances never freeze.** Opening tank balances, debts, loans, tickets and remainders stay editable for ever; only a payment or settlement against a debt or loan locks its amount (REVIEW 2026-10-20 B1). After go-live a typed correction changes every past figure, with the audit line as the only trace.

## C. Weaknesses that stay (decided or accepted earlier, listed so the decision is renewed knowingly)
- **C1. Password hashes. FIXED on the branch:** `Pull::row('users')` sends null for roles outside `permissions.pos_roles`. A terminal that already holds other roles' hashes drops them when that user next changes on the web, or after a full re-sync (`sync/begin`). The original finding: The users pull sends `pos_salt` and `pos_hash` of every user, including admin and manager, to every terminal; the terminal reads only cashiers. This was fixed on my deleted branch and is not on `main`. One change in `Pull::row('users')`: send null for roles outside `permissions.pos_roles`.
- **C2. Card PINs** are a salted SHA-256 of 4–8 digits, held by the web and every terminal; a copy of either gives every PIN. (PBKDF2 is available in Qt 5.3.)
- **C3. The converter port** accepts any client and a new one replaces the real link (`BoardBus::newConnection`). Accepted: closed LAN.
- **C4. Terminal clock.** No guard refuses a sale or supply when the clock is earlier than the newest record (a power cut with no RTC battery).
- **C5. Edit during upload.** `Uploader::replyFinished` marks a row synced by key only; an edit saved while the request is in flight is never uploaded. You asked me to skip this earlier.
- **C6. Defaults.** `admin` / `admin123`, machine PIN `0000`, `APP_DEBUG=true` in `.env.example`. You asked me to skip this earlier.
- **C7. Remainder rows purged before printing. FIXED on the branch:** `Purger::purge` and `removeConfirmed` keep a remainder whose ticket is still sold and unused, and the Remainder tickets list shows every such one however old. The original finding: `Purger::purge` deletes a synced remainder older than `purge_days` even if it was never printed or used. This was fixed on my deleted branch and is not on `main`.
- **C8. Settlements and remainders.** A ticket can settle two loans (web and terminal) and a remainder ticket can belong to two remainders; neither is checked on the push.

## D. Small
- A fuel inbound or outbound can be dated in the future, and an outbound by hand can take more than the tank holds (`OutboundController`, `FuelInboundController`).
- A supply of a nozzle with no tank leaves no outbound row and no warning (`FuelOutbound::syncSupplement`).
- A debt payment has no double-submit guard.
- A web delete of a terminal-origin sale followed by the confirmation purge lets a later terminal re-push recreate the sale as live.
- Ticket publish checks stock outside its transaction.
- `TankController::withCapacityWarning` finds the tank by the typed name; it is unique, so this is only fragile if the name is changed in the same save.

## E. Performance (fine for a year, then not)
`DebtController::debtsWithBalances`, `DebtSummaryController`, `BackupRun::settledDebts` / `settledLoans`, `TankCostLayers::all` and `CardLedger::recompute` load or walk whole tables per request, per backup, or per ledger entry. They need SQL aggregates before the data grows.
